## Owner-level calls

<!-- D9: calls the spec leaves open, with the options considered. -->

- **Shortcut:** `Ctrl+Alt+B` / `⌃⌥B` (D1's default; free in both apps). Considered `F9` alone (games often use F-keys) and `Ctrl+Shift+B` (build in many editors).
- **Stopping by shortcut:** the bug's stop time is the key press, but it's saved 1.5 s later so words still being recognized are kept. Considered stopping at once (loses the last sentence when the key follows it) and waiting for the recognizer to go quiet (unbounded).
- **Unsure commands:** a "bug start/stop/cancel" the microphone recognizer marks low-confidence is ignored (Windows: SAPI `SP_LOW_CONFIDENCE`), so game chatter doesn't open bugs. Considered acting on every match.
- **Notifications:** "Bug started" (with voice: "Describe it, then say “bug stop” (or press Ctrl+Alt+B)."; without: "Press Ctrl+Alt+B again to save it."), "Saving the bug…" on a shortcut stop, "Bug saved" (title, "The clip is cut when the recording stops." when pending, "Click to open the folder."), "Bug cancelled · Nothing was saved.", "Voice commands aren't available" / "Voice commands are off" with the reason and "the shortcut still makes bug reports".
- **Overlay look:** a full-width bar, black at 72%; header in bold at 62% of the text size, muted off-white with "BUG" in Ather lime; description semibold white, left-aligned; text size 3.4% of the picture's height, at most width/40, at least 14 px. Considered a centered caption pill like video captions (harder to read for several lines).
- **Nothing said:** title "Bug at 14:03:22"; the folder is just the date and time; bug.md says "Nothing: voice commands were off." or "Nothing was recognized: listen to `voice.wav`."
- **Gallery:** `bug.png`, `screenshot.png` and `clip.mp4` are tagged `bug`, with the description as their comment and the game as their app (the gallery lists every picture in the captures folder, so tagging `screenshot.png` too keeps a `bug` filter complete). Considered stacking `bug.png` on `screenshot.png` as an edit (adds an "edited" tag).
- **Issue note:** text "Bug: <description>" (or "Bug: <folder name>"), author "Bug report", from the bug's start to its stop on the recording.
- **Shared notes:** markers and bug notes now join an existing sidecar instead of skipping it (before, a second writer's markers were dropped with "couldn't be saved"); a sidecar that can't be read is still left alone.
- **voice.wav:** 16 kHz mono 16-bit (speech quality, about 2 MB a minute).
- **Logs:** "at most 20" per folder or wildcard entry; the kept tail starts at a line; a log still being written is copied up to the size it had when copying began.
- **Mac clip rate:** H.264 at about 0.75 MB a second of clip, between 4 and 50 MB (Windows uses the export's standard H.264 settings).
- **Quitting with a bug open (Windows):** it's saved, and quitting waits for it. **Copy last bug report** after a restart takes the newest folder in `Bugs`.
- **Mac settings rows:** the description goes into the row title (Mac rows have no hint line).
